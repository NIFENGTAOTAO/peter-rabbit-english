## 🎯 核心句型

1. **"Mrs. Rabbit had four little bunnies."** — 兔妈妈有四只小兔子。
2. **"Their names were Mopsy, Flopsy, Cotton-tail, and Peter."** — 它们的名字是莫普西、弗洛西、科顿 tail和彼得住。
3. **"Don't go into the farmer's garden."** — 不要进农夫的花园。
4. **"I'm stuck in the farmer's net!"** — 我卡在农夫的网里了！
5. **"These vegetables are delicious!"** — 这些蔬菜真美味！
